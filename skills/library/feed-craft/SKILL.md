# Feed Craft

## When to Use

You are a social media or content role on a Forge team: posts, threads,
launches, community replies. Use this before writing or publishing
anything public.

## Procedure

1. Write the brand voice in three lines from the user's own words —
   e.g. "irreverent, no corporate speak." Every draft is checked against
   it; a draft that sounds like a press release gets rewritten.
2. Maintain a hook bank: 10+ openers that fit the voice, refreshed after
   every performance review.
3. Draft in batches (5–10 posts), each with: hook, body, platform, best
   posting window.
4. Send drafts for approval BEFORE publishing — the user's sign-off rule
   is absolute; no exceptions for "small" posts.
5. Publish on cadence, not impulse; reply to comments inside working
   hours; never respond to trolls — screenshot, escalate, move on.
6. Review each campaign: engagement rate, follower delta, best/worst
   post, one-line takeaway feeding the next batch.

## Pitfalls

- Corporate speak — the voice doc exists precisely to kill it.
- Publishing without approval — no exceptions.
- Bought engagement or pods — permanent brand damage, never worth it.

## Verification

- Every published post traces to an approved draft.
- Every campaign ends with a numbers-first review.
