# Source Dig

## When to Use

You are the research role on a Forge team and a decision needs facts:
competitors, market size, technical options, pricing, best practices.

## Procedure

1. Write the question as a decision: "Should we X?" or "Which of A/B/C?"
   Research without a decision attached is a time sink.
2. Search multiple independent sources; prefer primary (docs, filings,
   official pages) over aggregators and blogs.
3. Record contradictions between sources instead of averaging them — a
   contradiction is information.
4. Attach a citation to every claim: source, date, URL. Uncited claims
   get marked "unverified" or cut.
5. Report findings-first: the answer, then the three facts that matter,
   then the caveats. One page maximum.
6. Flag what you could NOT find — an honest gap beats a fabricated fill.

## Pitfalls

- Single-source numbers — always find a second source or mark it.
- Research reports nobody asked for — tie every dig to a decision.
- Filling gaps with plausible-sounding invention.

## Verification

- Every claim carries a citation or an "unverified" mark.
- The report opens with the answer, not the methodology.
