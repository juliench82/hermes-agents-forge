# Hermes Agents Forge

**Hire a team of AI specialists in a single conversation.**

Hermes Agents Forge turns your HERMES agent into a hiring manager. Tell it what you need, approve the plan once — and it builds you a working team of AI specialists, each with its own identity, judgment, and real skills.

No setup. No configuration files. No prompt engineering.

## How it works

1. **Visit and click.** Open [the site](https://hermes-agents-forge.vercel.app) and hit "Read agent instructions."
2. **Have a conversation.** Your agent asks a few simple questions about your work, your tools, and what good results look like.
3. **Say yes — once.** Review the proposed team and approve it. Your agent then builds everything and hands you the keys.

That's the whole onboarding. One link, one conversation, one yes.

## What you get

A team built around you, not a template:

- **Specialists matched to your actual work.** A launch team for a founder, a QA team for a developer, a content team for a creator — the roles come from your answers, never from a fixed catalog.
- **Real personalities.** Each specialist gets a rich persona: its own mission, working style, boundaries, and rules — grounded in your own words, so it works the way you expect instead of guessing.
- **Real skills.** Each specialist is installed with working skills from the HERMES Skills Hub. No invented capabilities, no silent gaps — you get a full inventory of what every team member can do.
- **Proof it worked.** Every team comes verified: profile list, skill inventory, a smoke test per specialist, and a written team record you keep.
- **A team that talks to each other.** Your specialists message each other, share a group chat, and run routines — and every one of them knows when to escalate to you.

## Who it's for

- **Solo founders** who need a marketing team without hiring five people
- **Developers** who want research, QA, and DevOps help that actually coordinates
- **Creators** juggling content, community, and launches
- **Anyone with recurring workflows** they'd rather delegate than repeat

## Team sizes

| Package | Best for |
|---|---|
| **3 specialists** | One clear job, one domain |
| **5 specialists** | Several projects that need analysis and review |
| **7 specialists** | Complex operations with real coordination |

Your agent recommends the smallest team that covers your needs — you're never sold specialists you don't need.

## Trust and safety

- **Nothing is built before you approve the full plan** — one explicit yes, in plain language, covers everything that happens after.
- **Every third-party skill is security-scanned** before installation. Anything that doesn't pass never touches your machine.
- **Your browser, your accounts, your rules.** By default, specialists use your real browser profile — they work with your logins, like a colleague at your desk.
- **Clear boundaries.** Every specialist has written rules about what it will never do, and every team member knows how and when to escalate to you.

## Requirements

Just [HERMES](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/docs/) — the free, open-source personal agent from Nous Research. The Forge works with any model, any reasoning level; it's been proven end-to-end on a small local model with zero hand-holding.
