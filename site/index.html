03 / The method

Six steps.
One approval.

  1. 01

    Interview

    Five questions, asked in plain conversation. Your exact words are kept — they end up quoted inside your specialists' personas.

  2. 02

    Select tier

    Teams ship as 3, 5, or 7 specialists — never 4, never 6. The smallest tier that covers the work wins.

  3. 03

    Propose team

    You see the full roster before anything exists: each specialist's role, ownership, tools, and approval boundaries.

  4. 04

    One approval

    A single explicit yes authorizes the whole plan — team, personas, and skills. After that, no mid-flow questions.

  5. 05

    Provision

    The agent builds against that approval. On HERMES: isolated bot-mode profiles plus a decision bot — the only approval layer, and workers never approve each other.

  6. 06

    Verify

    Receipts, not assertions: the roster listing, a skill inventory per specialist, one smoke test each, and a written TEAM.md record.

No installation. The agent is the installer.
